# ADM Profile: `vehicle.car.v1`

**Status:** normative proposal for ADM Draft 0.4; not yet independently certified. **Applies to:** wheeled passenger cars and comparable four-or-more-wheel road vehicles. Three-wheelers, motorcycles, tracked vehicles and articulated trucks need other profiles. **Game logic:** expressly excluded.

## V1. Coordinate and semantic frame

ADM model coordinate frame (§2): right-handed, +X vehicle RIGHT, +Y UP, -Z vehicle FORWARD. Origins, transforms and all lengths in meters. The profile's frame is the root scene frame and MUST NOT be inferred from mesh bounds. Positive yaw about +Y turns the vehicle's forward vector toward -X (left). Positive roll about +X moves the bottom of a wheel toward -Z (forward). Each role is bound to exactly one scene node using `componentRoles`, with `profile:"vehicle.car.v1"`. Bind nodes MUST have TRS (not `matrix`), finite values, positive scale, and be unique among bound roles. Profile kinematic pivots MUST have scale [1,1,1], rotation identity and their local axes aligned with profile axes at rest; mesh geometry may be under a child node with its own transform. Do not mirror node scales to make left/right variants; mirror mesh data before export. Every bound role has exactly one semantic node, though that node may have arbitrary descendants. The mesh in a role subtree is not necessarily the role node itself.

The model MUST declare `modelProfiles:[{"id":"vehicle.car.v1","schemaVersion":1,"metadata":{...}}]`. Exactly one `vehicle.car.v1` record per file. Metadata JSON field names and types are case-sensitive. Unknown metadata keys are invalid (except a namespaced `extensions` object under `metadata`). A model claiming this profile MUST have `componentRoles` entries as below and conform to all geometry and transform constraints.

## V2. Required and optional roles

Required: `chassis`, `wheel.axle0.left`, `wheel.axle0.right`, `wheel.axle1.left`, `wheel.axle1.right`. Axles are indexed front to rear, starting at 0; at least 2 axles. All wheels on a given axle MUST share its axle index. Each axle MUST contain a left and a right wheel; additional per-axle wheels are not part of v1 and require a versioned profile extension. Any number of axles >=2 is valid. Wheel positions are computed in chassis-local coordinates from the composed bind-pose node transforms, not from immediate local translations. No fixed chassis-height requirement exists. Axle order MUST have strictly decreasing Z coordinates: the mean of the left/right wheel centers on axle i has larger Z for i+1 (front is -Z); hence `z(axle0) < z(axle1) < ...` (strictly increasing Z). Left and right wheel centers on the same axle MUST have finite position and `left.x < right.x`. Wheels MUST have distinct role node indices. `chassis` MUST be an ancestor of each wheel pivot and other kinematic nodes.

Optional singular: `steering.wheel`, `door.front.left`, `door.front.right`, `door.rear.left`, `door.rear.right`, `hood`, `trunk`, `light.front.left`, `light.front.right`, `light.rear.left`, `light.rear.right`, `seat.driver`, `seat.passenger.front`, `suspension.axleN.left`, `suspension.axleN.right`, `steering.axleN.left`, `steering.axleN.right`. `N` MUST denote an existing axle index as unpadded base-10. Additional rear rows and custom mechanical parts MUST use qualified custom roles (e.g. `org.example.vehicle.roof_panel`) and are not standard v1 semantics. Lights and seats are static marker transforms unless explicitly animated. None implies runtime lighting, occupants or physics.

## V3. Kinematic hierarchy, pivot rules and properties

Wheel hub pivot origin equals modeled wheel center in rest pose. The wheel spin axis is **local +X**, both sides (not reversed on the left). Its local +Y is up and -Z is vehicle forward at zero steering. Every wheel role is the **spin** pivot. If wheel steering is supported, wheel role MUST be a direct or indirect descendant of the matching `steering.axleN.side` pivot, and no unrelated rotational pivot may intervene; `steering` rotates about local +Y, wheel rotates about local +X. If suspension articulation is present, the hierarchy MUST be `chassis → suspension pivot → steering pivot (if present) → wheel spin pivot`, allowing inert mesh nodes between named pivots only if their rest transform is identity. `suspension` translation is local +Y (positive raises hub). Steering positive angles rotate -Z towards -X. Steering wheel (interior) rotates about its own local +Z, positive is counter-clockwise from looking along +Z toward origin; the steering ratio is **not** encoded by this profile. Hinge roles `door.*`, `hood`, `trunk` have pivot at the modeled real hinge; each rotates about its local +Y (doors) or +X (hood, trunk). A role's metadata gives `openAngleRad`, signed radians from rest to fully open. The source pose is closed; to open to fraction `u∈[0,1]`, apply `R_local(u)=R_rest × axisAngle(localAxis,u×openAngleRad)`. No required convention for sign of opening because hinge direction depends on geometry; the sign MUST be explicit, never guessed from side. Models claiming a door/hood/trunk role MUST supply the corresponding `hinges` metadata. Each hinge angle MUST be finite and nonzero with `|angle|<=pi`. For steering `steeringAngleRad` is a maximum absolute mechanical visual limit in [0,pi/2]; clipping driver input is outside ADM. `wheelRadius` and `wheelWidth` are measured from intended collision/visual wheel envelope, not auto-inferred from geometry. Radius>0, width>0; collision simulation is out of scope.

`metadata` MUST contain `axles`, ordered by `index` ascending, with unique consecutive indexes `0..N-1`. Each entry `{ "index":u32, "wheels":{"left":{"role":str,"radius":f32,"width":f32},"right":{...}}, "steeringAngleRad"?:f32, "driven"?:bool }`. Wheel role strings MUST be `wheel.axle<index>.<side>`; `steeringAngleRad` default zero and signals visual steering capability (nonzero requires matching `steering.axleN.*` roles for both sides). `driven` default false is descriptive only; it is not a torque or drivetrain specification. Every declared wheel role MUST appear in exactly one axle entry. `hinges` optional list `{ "role":str,"openAngleRad":f32 }` with unique role strings, one for every present door/hood/trunk role and no absent role. `visualOnly`: optional bool, default false; a true value documents a static decorative model and does not relax mandatory axle roles. No other metadata keys are defined in v1.

## V4. Assembly, sockets, animation and collision

Optional sockets are standard ADM §24 attachments. Recommended names: `mount.wheel.axleN.left`, `mount.wheel.axleN.right`, `seat.driver`, `seat.passenger.front`, `mount.body.front`, `mount.body.rear`; a name MUST resolve to exactly one node. A socket is **only** a frame: no automatic dependency resolution, spawning or part-swapping. For a plug-in wheel asset, the wheel asset root has a `mount.root` attachment at its intended hub frame. Host alignment uses `W_childRoot = W_parentSocket × inverse(L_childMount)`, where L_childMount is mount transform relative to the child root; no automatic scale fitting. Host must reject incompatible attachment categories declared by a versioned assembly extension rather than guess. Exact role/sockets survive export/import by string identity.

A vehicle model MAY provide a matching `.adm_anim` library with `targetProfile:"vehicle.car.v1"`, `binding:"SEMANTIC"`. Standard mechanical animation channels use `target.kind:"component"`, `target.role` and paths `rotation` (F32 VEC4 quaternion) or `translation` (F32 VEC3); sampled values are **deltas relative to role node rest TRS**, not absolute transforms. Apply translation as `T_rest + deltaT` (parent-space vector); rotation as `Q_rest × deltaQ` (local-space rotation). Scale animations are disallowed for semantic mechanical components v1. Component roles must be unique; missing required channels reject binding unless channel `optional:true`. Cross-model semantic compatibility guarantees axis and pivot interpretation, **not** identical model geometry or motion ranges. Actual animation values exceeding hinge or steering limits MUST be rejected for `vehicle.car.v1` clips when the corresponding role and metadata are bound. Wheel spin is unbounded by design (continuous rotations represented as sampled quaternions). A reference animation `vehicle@car@inspection:doors_open` at t=1 s shall open each declared left/right front door to u=1, with sampled rotations equal to the angle metadata in signed direction. A reference clip `vehicle@car@wheels:roll` may animate wheel spin; any motion of the car in world space remains host responsibility.

Colliders (§22) MAY approximate chassis/wheels; they cannot prescribe rigid-body dynamics. Collision node and `physicalMaterial` bindings are model metadata. LOD variants MUST NOT change the semantic role-node mappings or pivot frames; LOD switching only swaps visual geometry. A viewer MAY render wheel rotations without a physics engine and MUST expose missing or unbound role diagnostics.

## V5. Conformance tests (normative design; executable fixtures still pending)

- `VEH-001`: reject fewer than two axles, missing wheel side, duplicate/out-of-range axle index, wrong wheel role, or missing chassis ancestor.
- `VEH-002`: assert wheel local spin +X: rotation +π/2 maps bottom vector (0,-r,0) to (0,0,-r) in wheel-local rest frame.
- `VEH-003`: positive steering yaw +π/6 maps forward (0,0,-1) to (-0.5,0,-√3/2) within 1e-5; no geometry/physics needed.
- `VEH-004`: door `openAngleRad=+π/2` results from u=1 in Q_rest×rotY(+π/2), no side-dependent guess.
- `VEH-005`: reject LOD/assembly role frame drift; check wheel hub mount pairing W_childRoot formula.
- `VEH-006`: load two distinct car models with different axle widths but same valid semantic animation; ensure role names, frame axes and computed delta rotations agree.

All comparisons use §25 numeric tolerances unless stricter values are explicitly specified above. A parser-only reader may validate metadata but MUST NOT claim full `ADM-VEHICLE-CAR-1` animation conformance unless kinematic evaluation is implemented and fixture-tested.
